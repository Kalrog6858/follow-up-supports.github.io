What this is

A free, simple guide to youth services

The Youth Resource Centre is a free online guide for youth and the workers who support them. It brings together trusted housing, food, health, mental health, legal, and employment services in Toronto and across Ontario, so finding help takes seconds instead of hours.

It’s especially for young people moving out of shelters or care and into their own place, a time when knowing where to turn makes all the difference.

Who it’s for

  • Youth aged 16–24
  • Young people leaving shelter, care, or unstable housing
  • Shelter, outreach, and housing staff looking for referrals
  • Anyone who needs free support in Ontario
🔎

How resources are chosen

Every listing links to an official public service or established community organization. Most are free and open to all.

🤝

Our approach

Person-centred, trauma-informed, and strengths-based: meeting each youth where they are and focusing on their goals and safety.

🔄

Always changing

Programs open, close, and move. We update this site as things change, but please call ahead before visiting.

Our philosophy

Every young person has the right to safety, dignity, and opportunity. Healing and growth happen through relationships built on trust and consistency. Each youth is the expert in their own story, and our role is to support them in writing their next chapter.
