Welcome

A starting point for whatever comes next.

This website was made to help youth move forward after leaving shelters or care. It connects you to real housing, food, health, and employment supports across Ontario. Our goal is to make the next steps easier and give every young person a fair chance to grow and succeed.

What you’ll find here

  • Verified services across the GTA and Ontario
  • Practical info for housing, income, legal help, and health
  • A living map we update as programs change
