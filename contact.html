Crisis & support lines

988 Suicide Crisis Helpline For anyone thinking about suicide, or worried about someone. 24/7, call or text. 📞 988
Kids Help Phone Free counselling for young people, any time. Or text CONNECT to 686868. 📞 1-800-668-6868
Gerstein Crisis Centre 24/7 mental health crisis line for adults (16+) in Toronto. 📞 416-929-5200
Distress Centres of Greater Toronto 24/7 emotional support, crisis and suicide prevention. 📞 416-408-4357
Hope for Wellness 24/7 counselling and crisis support for Indigenous people. 📞 1-855-242-3310
Assaulted Women’s Helpline 24/7 support for women experiencing abuse. 📞 1-866-863-0511
Trans Lifeline Peer support run by and for trans people. 📞 1-877-330-6366
LGBT YouthLine Text-based peer support for 2SLGBTQ+ youth in Ontario. 💬 Text 647-694-4275

Toronto Shelter Central Intake 24/7. Find an emergency shelter bed in Toronto. 📞 416-338-4766
211 Ontario 24/7 help finding any community, social or government service in Ontario. 📞 211
ConnexOntario 24/7 help finding mental health, addiction and gambling services. 📞 1-866-531-2600
Toronto Police (non-emergency) To report something that isn’t an emergency. 📞 416-808-2222
